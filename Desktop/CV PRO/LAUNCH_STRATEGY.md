## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Server downtime | Multi-region deployment, auto-failover |
| Data loss | Daily backups, disaster recovery plan |
| Security breach | Penetration testing, bug bounty program |
| Performance issues | Load testing, CDN caching, optimization |

### Business Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Low user adoption | Strong onboarding, product-market fit |
| Churn rate high | Great retention, premium features |
| Competitor entry | First-mover advantage, strong UX |
| Funding needed | Conservative spending, focus on revenue |

### Market Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Job market slowdown | Expand to freelance, gig economy |
| LinkedIn competition | Differentiate: AI, interview prep |
| Regulatory changes | GDPR/CCPA compliant from day 1 |
